Customer Service

Great customer service is about making every guest feel welcomed, respected and cared for throughout their experience.

What is good customer service?

Customer service is more than simply taking orders and serving food. It is about understanding what guests need, communicating clearly and creating a positive experience from the moment they arrive until they leave.

The Five Principles of Good Service

1. Welcome
Greet guests warmly and make them feel comfortable from the beginning of their visit.
2. Listen
Pay attention to what guests say and make sure you understand their needs before responding.
3. Communicate
Give clear and polite information. If you are unsure, check with a colleague or manager rather than guessing.
4. Stay Calm
When a guest is unhappy, remain calm and professional. Focus on understanding the problem instead of arguing.
5. Follow Up
After helping a guest, check that the problem has been resolved and that they are satisfied.

Handling a Complaint

Complaints should be taken seriously. The goal is not to prove who is right or wrong. The goal is to understand the guest's concern and find an appropriate solution.

EXAMPLE

A guest says that their food has been taking too long. Instead of immediately explaining that the kitchen is busy, listen to the guest, apologize politely and check the order status.

Key Point

Listen → Apologize → Check → Respond

When You Don't Know the Answer

You do not need to know everything. However, you should avoid giving incorrect information. If you are unsure about a guest's question, tell them that you will check and then find the correct answer.

Remember

Never guess. Check and confirm.
