Food Safety

Food safety is essential for protecting guests, maintaining food quality and preventing contamination in the restaurant.

Why is food safety important?

Every restaurant has a responsibility to serve food that is safe to eat. Good food safety practices help prevent contamination, reduce health risks and protect both guests and staff.

The Five Principles of Food Safety

1. Clean
Keep hands, surfaces, equipment and work areas clean. Wash hands regularly and follow the restaurant's cleaning procedures.
2. Separate
Keep raw and cooked foods separate to reduce the risk of cross-contamination.
3. Cook
Cook food properly and follow the restaurant's procedures for safe cooking and preparation.
4. Keep Food at Safe Temperatures
Store and hold food at appropriate temperatures to help maintain quality and reduce food safety risks.
5. Report Problems
If you notice unsafe food, damaged equipment, poor hygiene or another food safety problem, report it to a manager immediately.

Hand Washing

Proper hand washing is one of the most important ways to prevent the spread of germs and contamination. Hands should be washed regularly, especially before handling food and whenever contamination may occur.

EXAMPLE

Before preparing or serving food, wash your hands properly. If you touch something dirty or potentially contaminated, wash your hands again before continuing.

Key Point

Clean hands → Safe food → Protected guests

Preventing Cross-Contamination

Cross-contamination can happen when harmful bacteria or other contaminants are transferred from one food, surface or piece of equipment to another. Keep raw and cooked foods separate and follow the restaurant's procedures for food preparation and storage.

Remember

Keep raw food and ready-to-eat food separate.

When You Notice a Food Safety Problem

Do not ignore food safety problems. If you notice something that may make food unsafe, stop and report the issue to a manager. Following the correct procedure is more important than simply finishing the task quickly.

EXAMPLE

You notice that a food item has been left out for too long. Do not simply continue serving it. Inform the responsible manager and follow the restaurant's food safety procedure.
