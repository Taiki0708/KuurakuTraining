Restaurant Basics

Learn the fundamental knowledge and everyday practices needed to work effectively, safely and confidently in a restaurant.

The Basics of Restaurant Work

Working in a restaurant requires more than serving food. Every team member plays an important role in creating a smooth and positive guest experience.

The basics include cleanliness, communication, teamwork, safety and professional service.

Key point: Great restaurants are built by great teamwork.

Before Service

Prepare Your Station

Before service begins, make sure your work area is clean, organized and ready.

  • Check that tables are clean.
  • Prepare necessary utensils and equipment.
  • Make sure menus and other items are ready.
  • Check that your station has everything you need.

Check Your Appearance

A professional appearance helps create a positive first impression.

  • Wear a clean uniform.
  • Keep your hands clean.
  • Maintain good personal hygiene.
  • Follow the restaurant's appearance standards.

During Service

Welcome Guests

Guests should be welcomed warmly and promptly when they arrive.

A warm greeting helps guests feel comfortable from the beginning.

Serve Carefully

  • Check the order before serving.
  • Handle dishes and drinks carefully.
  • Pay attention to food temperature.
  • Keep the table organized.

Communicate With Your Team

Good communication prevents mistakes and keeps service running smoothly.

  • Share important information with teammates.
  • Ask for help when necessary.
  • Confirm unclear requests.
  • Support teammates during busy periods.

Handling Guest Problems

When a guest has a problem, stay calm and professional.

  • Listen carefully.
  • Do not argue with the guest.
  • Apologize when appropriate.
  • Find a solution or ask a manager for help.
  • Follow up when possible.
Key point: Focus on solving the problem, not winning the argument.

Safety and Cleanliness

Cleanliness and safety are everyone's responsibility.

  • Wash your hands regularly.
  • Keep work areas clean.
  • Clean spills quickly.
  • Handle hot food and equipment carefully.
  • Report unsafe situations immediately.

Teamwork

Restaurant service is a team effort. A strong team communicates, supports each other and shares responsibility.

  • Help teammates when they are busy.
  • Communicate clearly.
  • Respect other team members.
  • Take responsibility for your work.
  • Keep a positive attitude.
Key point: When the team works well together, guests have a better experience.

End of Service

Good work continues until the restaurant is ready for the next service.

  • Clean your station.
  • Return equipment and supplies to the correct place.
  • Complete required closing tasks.
  • Report problems or shortages.
  • Leave the workplace ready for the next team.
